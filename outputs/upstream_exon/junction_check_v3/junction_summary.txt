2. DIRECTION OF THE EFFECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  with    >=1 cryptic donor: 3.4620 +- 0.5884
  without  0 cryptic donors: 3.1941 +- 0.6409
  Mann-Whitney U p = 8.188e-28
  cryptic donors go WITH a HIGHER target, the opposite of the artifact
  worry, which was that they compete splicing away from the middle exon.

  The middle exon's TWO sites rise together (see section 5): the
  acceptor by +0.71 logit and the donor by +0.63 across 0 -> 3 cryptic
  donors. An acceptor-specific mechanism would lift the acceptor far
  more, since the donor at 318 lies downstream of the middle exon. So
  this is exon-level recognition, not a cryptic donor feeding one site;
  whether the cryptic donor causes that or merely co-occurs with B1
  sequence that strengthens the whole cassette is NOT resolved here.
